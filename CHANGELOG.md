# Changelog

## [0.1.1] - 2026-10-21
- Cab glass is easier to see through, especially from inside the cab.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-19
First release, for Bedrock and Java (Fabric 26.3):
- 12 machines you drive and operate: excavator, wheel loader, bulldozer, articulated dump truck, skid steer, crawler crane, mobile crane, tower crane, concrete mixer truck, road roller, forklift and telehandler.
- Real terrain work: buckets dig and dump, dump trucks tip, bulldozers level and push, the road roller makes paths and the mixer pours concrete in any colour.
- Cranes lift blocks, animals and other machines up to their load limit. The tower crane is built on a mast of crane mast blocks with the Tower Crane Kit.
- Java: operating keys (arrows, I/K, U/J, G) with an on-screen control panel. Bedrock: hotbar modes (slot 1 drive, 2 arm, 3 tool) with Jump as the action.
- Machines never touch bedrock, obsidian, portals or blocks with inventories, and terrain editing can be turned off.
- Machine Manual, recipes, English and Danish.
